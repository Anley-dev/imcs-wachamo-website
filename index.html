Who We Are

Living the Faith on Campus

IMCS Pax Romana Wachemo is a community of young Catholic intellectuals striving to integrate deep spiritual life with high academic excellence.

Our Mission

To witness to the Gospel of Christ within the higher education ecosystem, fostering spiritual, intellectual, and human growth among students.

Our Vision

To build a generation of dynamic Catholic leaders equipped with integrity, professional competence, and a deep sense of social responsibility.

Our Core Values

Grounded in spiritual solidarity, intellectual depth, sacrificial service, and a global community framework with Catholic teaching.

Our Foundation

Core Principles

Rooted in Catholic faith, we stand for authentic discipleship and transformative action

Spiritual Growth

Deepening our relationship with Christ through prayer, sacraments, and formation. We believe personal holiness fuels effective service.

Community Building

Creating authentic brotherhoods and sisterhoods where young people find belonging, friendship, and mutual support in faith.

Social Transformation

Living the Gospel's call to justice and charity. We serve the poor, advocate for dignity, and work toward a more just world.
